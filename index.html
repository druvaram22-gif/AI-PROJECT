A few things to know

Travel well, travel easy

🗓️ When to go

October to March is pleasant for most destinations. The monsoon turns the Ghats lush and waterfalls spectacular.

🚆 Getting around

Bengaluru is the main gateway. Trains and buses connect major cities; taxis help with rural areas.

🧭 Good to know

Kannada is the state language. English is widely understood in cities. Carry cash in smaller towns.

🌱 Travel thoughtfully

Respect temple customs, take litter with you and choose local guides and homestays when possible.
